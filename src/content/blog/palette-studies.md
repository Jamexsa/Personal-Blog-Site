Between programming sessions I've been doing palette studies — small
environment thumbnails painted with exactly four colors plus white. The
constraint is doing more design work than any mood board ever has.

## Why four colors

Fewer colors force value structure to carry the composition. When you can't
reach for a new hue, you start asking what the light is doing, and the
thumbnails suddenly have depth instead of decoration.

The working rules:

- One dark, one mid, one saturated accent, one surprise color
- The accent only appears where I want the eye to go
- White is reserved for light sources and edges

## Where this is going

The recurring winner across a dozen studies is a charcoal base with a single
warm coral accent — which, conveniently, is already this site's palette. The
game and the portfolio sharing a visual language was unintentional, but it
feels right: same author, same instincts.

Next studies move from thumbnails to character silhouettes, still under the
four-color rule.
