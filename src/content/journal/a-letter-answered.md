Answered a letter that had been waiting since the spring — not because the
words were hard, but because the good ones kept arriving late.

Sealed it with the crooked wax stamp (the one that always leans), walked it to
the post cart, and felt the particular lightness of a debt paid to no one but
myself. Some days the quest is a stamp and a walk. Those count.
