Bought a bag of roasted chestnuts, traded gossip with the candle-maker, and
watched a very confident goose chase a very confident knight. The goose won.

Came home with chestnuts, one bruised apple, and a small polished stone that
looked like a rune but is definitely just a stone. It sits on the desk now.
The desk did not need it. The desk has it anyway.
