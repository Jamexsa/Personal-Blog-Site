A day of half-finished things: a sketch abandoned at the inking stage, a tune
played three-quarters through, a shelf mounted slightly crooked on purpose so
it would stop bothering me.

Rain helps. When the roof drums like that, sitting still feels like a choice
instead of a failure. Tomorrow the sketch gets finished or buried. No verdicts
after dark.
