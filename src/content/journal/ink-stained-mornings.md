Woke before the bell again. The fog was so thick the courtyard looked like a
blank parchment — fitting, since that is roughly what my schedule was today.

Filled three inkwells, sharpened the good quill, and wrote nothing of note for
two hours. Then the good line arrived all at once and the morning redeemed
itself. Note to self: keep the ink filled even on the empty days.
