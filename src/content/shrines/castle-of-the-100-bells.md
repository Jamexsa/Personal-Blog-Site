## Why it matters

Every background is a painting that decided to keep moving. I have watched the
market-day sequence more times than any reasonable person should admit, and I
still find a new tiny animation in it — a dog, a flag, a stolen pie — every
time.

## Favorite trinkets

- Bells as chapter markers: the film is over when you have heard them all.
- The palette: six colors, sworn enemies, forced to cooperate.
- The sound of the crowd scenes, which is somehow also a color.

## Relics kept here

The bell-tower silhouettes on this site's wallpaper are a distant cousin of
its art direction — a tribute drawn in my own hand, not a copy.
