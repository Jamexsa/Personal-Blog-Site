## Why it matters

I played it during a rainy week and finished it with the window still open so
the real rain could keep score. Nothing in it can be rushed, nothing can be
lost permanently, and the only failure state is giving up on a light.

## Favorite trinkets

- The map that fills in only where your lantern has actually been.
- The mail system between lighthouse keepers — letters arrive late, on purpose.
- The ending, which is a choice about which lamps you leave burning.

## Relics kept here

A palette study of the drowned coast hangs in the gallery, and the weather
system in my own prototypes owes this game more than a small debt.
